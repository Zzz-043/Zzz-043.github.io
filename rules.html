Five Main Events

  • Men's Singles
    One player against one player
  • Women's Singles
    One player against one player
  • Men's Doubles
    A team of two male players
  • Women's Doubles
    A team of two female players
  • Mixed Doubles
    One male and one female player

Scoring System

Matches use the best-of-three format. The first side to reach 21 points wins a game. At 20-all, a side must lead by two points to win. At 29-all, the first side to reach 30 points wins.

The Olympic Stage

Badminton became an official Olympic sport at the 1992 Barcelona Games and is now one of the world's most widely watched racket sports.
