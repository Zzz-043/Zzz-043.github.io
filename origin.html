The Origin of Badminton

Origin Rules and Events Features Back to Home

Where the Name Came From

Badminton takes its name from Badminton House in Gloucestershire, England. Around 1873, British officers encountered and adapted the sport there, helping establish its modern form.

An Earlier Game

The modern sport developed from an old game in Poona, India. Its original rules and equipment were more recreational, and they became increasingly standardized as British players helped spread the sport.

Across the World

Badminton spread rapidly across Commonwealth countries and was refined through international competitions. Similar shuttlecock games also existed in ancient China, reflecting a worldwide interest in racket and shuttle games.
